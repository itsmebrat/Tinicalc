CALCULATOR INSTRUCTIONS

FILE
calc2.html - 32 bytes (on your Desktop)

HOW TO USE
1. Double-click calc2.html to open it in your browser.
2. Click the bar and type your math, for example: 12*3+4
3. Click outside the bar to get the answer. The bar now shows 40.
4. You can keep going: click the bar, type *2 after the answer, and click outside again.

WHAT YOU CAN TYPE
+   add             5+3
-   subtract        9-4
*   multiply        6*7
/   divide          20/4
%   remainder       17%5
**  power           2**10
( ) brackets        (2+3)*4

EXTRA (type exactly as shown)
Math.sqrt(144)      square root
Math.round(4.6)     round
Math.max(3,9,4)     biggest number
Math.PI             pi

NOTES
- Use * for multiply (not x) and / for divide.
- Empty bar + click away shows "undefined". Just clear it and retype.
- Anything unreadable (like letters) gives no answer. Clear and retype.
- Decimals can be slightly off: 0.1+0.2 gives 0.30000000000000004.
- Trig functions use radians, not degrees.
- Only open calculator files you made yourself, since they run any code typed in.
